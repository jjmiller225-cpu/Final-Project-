# 04 | From Findings to Action

**Purpose:** Connect the visual analysis and predictive model to practical public-health decisions.

## Document
- `Business Care Report - BUS 446.docx` — the complete submitted report, including its embedded charts and detailed discussion.

## Recommendations in the team's report
1. **Follow up with repeat violators** rather than relying solely on routine rotation.
2. **Evaluate an inspection-dispatch cutoff of 0.30**, measuring the trade-off between additional inspections and avoided missed adverse outcomes.
3. **Make refrigeration checks a pre-summer priority** to address temperature-control risk.

## Business case
The public-health challenge is resource allocation: inspectors cannot visit every food establishment with equal frequency. An interpretable risk-based approach may direct limited inspection capacity toward higher-risk locations, provided that model outputs support—not replace—inspector judgment.

## Scope and caution
Observed inspection outcomes are not foodborne illness outcomes. Findings are associations from a local, limited-period dataset. The cutoff proposal requires independent validation.
