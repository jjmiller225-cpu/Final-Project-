# 02 | Discovering the Risk Patterns

**Purpose:** Translate a large inspection dataset into readable evidence for operational decisions.

## Workbooks
- `Project for class-1.twb` — establishment types, inspection outcomes, and category/inspection-type comparisons.
- `Projecct for class Q2.twb` — critical food safety checks, facility-category patterns, and seasonal analysis.

## Visuals
See the `figures/` folder for figures extracted from the submitted final business report. The original Tableau workbooks are editable but may prompt for a local data connection.

## Three stories in the charts
1. **Where inspections turn adverse:** Middle schools, restaurants, and carry-out sites had elevated observed rates.
2. **What repeatedly goes wrong:** Cold holding (19.9%) and rodent/insect conditions (15.5%) dominate the team-reported noncompliance rankings.
3. **When hazards worsen:** Cold-holding noncompliance was higher in summer than winter.

## How to open
Install Tableau Desktop, download both `.twb` files and the Excel data workbook from Section 01, then update the workbook's data-source path to the local spreadsheet if requested. A `.twb` saves workbook structure, not the full underlying data.
